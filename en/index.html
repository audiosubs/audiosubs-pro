How it works

Download and install

Install the Windows installer. On first transcription the app downloads the AI model (once, ~220 MB).

Paste a URL or pick a file

A YouTube, Vimeo, TED, Twitch or Dailymotion link — or any local video/audio file.

Choose what to extract

Subtitles, audio or both. Languages: original, PT-BR, EN-US, ES.

Done!

Get your .srt (subtitles) and/or .mp3 (audio) files. Use them in your video editor.

FAQ

Do I need internet to use it?

Processing is 100% local (privacy guaranteed). You only need internet to download the video/subtitles from a URL and to download the AI model the first time.

Do my videos leave my computer?

No. All audio, transcription and translation processing happens on your machine. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

How long does transcription take?

It depends on the video length and your PC. Short videos take seconds; 1-hour videos can take a few minutes on CPU.

What is the 7-day trial?

On install you get 7 days of PRO free — unlimited conversions with no length limit. After that, you fall back to free (2×3 min/day) or buy PRO.

How do I activate my PRO key?

Open the app, click "Activate license" on the PRO banner and paste the key you received by email after purchase. Done, you're PRO instantly.

Can I use it on more than one PC?

Each license is valid on one PC. If you change machines, email support to transfer the activation.

Is this allowed? Doesn't it violate YouTube's terms?

AudioSubs extracts audio and subtitles for personal and research use, like an offline reader. It's your responsibility to only process content you have the right to.
