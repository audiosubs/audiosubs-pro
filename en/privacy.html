Privacy Policy

Last updated: 02/08/2026

1. Processing is local

AudioSubs processes audio, transcription and translation entirely on your computer. No video, audio or transcribed text is sent to our servers.

2. What is stored on your PC

The app only keeps local settings: language preferences, your license status (free/trial/PRO) and daily usage counters. This data lives on your disk in %APPDATA%\audiosubs\config.json and the Windows Registry.

3. What is sent to the internet

  • When you use a URL: the app downloads the public video/audio from that URL (same as opening the link in a browser).
  • AI model: on first transcription the app downloads the Whisper model (~220 MB) from a model server (Hugging Face).
  • Translation: translation uses a public translation endpoint. For 100% offline use, keep the "original" language.
  • License activation: the PRO key is validated locally; no personal information is sent to activate.

4. Purchase data

Purchases are processed by third-party platforms (Gumroad for international payments and Hotmart for Brazil). AudioSubs only receives the buyer's email to issue the license key. We never have access to card data.

5. Data not collected

We do not collect telemetry, anonymous usage, URL history, transcribed content or biometric data. No accounts, no signup required to use the app.

6. Rights and contact

For any privacy question or to request deletion of purchase data, write to audiosubspro@gmail.com.
